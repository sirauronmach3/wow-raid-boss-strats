### Raid overview
* #### [Voidspire](./voidspire/)
  - This is the big main raid for season 1
  - Located in Voidstorm
  - Timetable:
    * March 17:
      * LFR Wing 1
      * Normal
      * Heroic
    * March 24:
      * LFR Wing 2
      * Story Mode
      * Mythic
    * March 31:
      * LFR Wing 3
  - Bosses:
    * [Imperator Averzian](./voidspire/Imperator_Averzian.md)
    * [Vorasius](./voidspire/Vorasius.md) (Tier token: Hands)
    * [Fallen-King Salhadaar](./voidspire/Fallen-King_Slhadaar.md) (Tier token: Shoulder)
    * [Vaelgor & Ezzorak](./voidspire/Vaelgor_and_Ezzorak.md) (Tier token: Legs)
    * [Lightblinded Vanguard](./voidspire/Linghtblinded_Vanguard.md) (Tier token: Head)
    * [Crown of the Cosmos](./voidspire/Crown_of_the_Cosmos.md.md)

* #### [Dreamrift](./dreamrift/) - 
  - Single boss : [Chimaerus the Undreamt God](./dreamrift/Chimaerus.md)
  - Tier token : Chest
  - *Timetable*:
   (no story mode)
    * March 17:
      * LFR
      * Normal
      * Heroic
    * March 24:
      * Mythic


* #### [March on Quel'Danas](./march_on_queldanas/) - 
  - Located on Isle of Quel'Danas
  - Waiting several weeks
  - Bosses:
    * [Belo'ren, Child of Al'ar](./march_on_queldanas/Belo-ren.md)
    * [Midnight Falls](./march_on_queldanas/Midnight_Falls.md) (Tier token: universal)
  - *Timetable*:
    * March 31:
      * Normal
      * Heroic
      * Mythic
    * April 7:
      * LFR
      * Story Mode
