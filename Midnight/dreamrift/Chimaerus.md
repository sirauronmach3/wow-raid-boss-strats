# <img src="https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/journal/ui-ej-boss-malformed-manifestation.png"> Chimaerus 
This boss fight involves sending half the raid into an alternate version of the map (The Downstairs). The downstairs pops the shields on the adds which transports them to the normal world (Upstairs).

The boss will try to eat any [Coalesced Manifestations](#coalesced-manifestations).

# Abilities
- ## <img src="https://static.icy-veins.com/images/wow/large_icons/ability_earthen_azeritesurge.jpg" width=25> [Alndust Upheaval](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1262305).
  - Massive physical AoE attack centered on current tank.
  - Applies [Alnsight]() 
    - Allows players to see into the Aln and attack Manifestations
    - This is how you go "Downstairs"
  - Deals heavy physical damage split amongst everyone inside.
  - Appropriate group stacks on Tank
  - ***After*** the strike and the debuff is applied: 
    - tank swap
    - Affected groups go to other end to deal with [Colossal Horror](#colossal-horror) and other adds
